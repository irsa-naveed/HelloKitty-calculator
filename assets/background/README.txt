Put your own background picture in this folder and name it:

    background.jpg   (or .jpeg / .png / .webp / .gif)

The app picks it up automatically on the next run/refresh.
You can also upload one from the sidebar while the app is running.

Use an image you have the rights to (your own artwork, a licensed
wallpaper, a photo, etc.). Landscape images around 1920x1080 look best.
Big files are shrunk automatically to keep the app fast.
